How to play

  • Two action points a turn, on a turn timer. Blue tiles cost 1 AP, yellow dash tiles cost 2. Tap a tile to preview cover, tap again to move.
  • Full cover blocks 40 aim, half cover 20. Shots inside the graze band deal half damage instead of missing outright.
  • High ground (raised platforms) gives +15 aim, +2 sight, and lets you shoot over half cover. Climbing costs 1 extra move.
  • Buildings with interiors have doors and windows. Windows are half cover you can see through. A closed door is full cover and blocks sight; walking through one opens it (+1 move). Next to a door you can open or close it (free, once a turn) or Breach it (1 AP: smashes it and stuns enemies right behind it).
  • Cover breaks. A missed shot at someone behind crates, cars, fences, windows or sandbags hits the cover instead (half the weapon’s minimum damage), and you can shoot objects directly (never misses, 1 ammo). At 0 HP full cover drops to half cover and half cover to rubble. Grenades knock everything in the blast down a stage.
  • Red fuel barrels and white fuel tanks explode when destroyed, hurting everyone nearby and setting off other explosives in the blast. Shoot them when an enemy hides next to one.
  • Return fire: an operative in full cover from its attacker shoots back once per enemy turn (reaction shot, −15 aim, 1 ammo) if it survives, can see the shooter and has ammo. Not while downed, disoriented, hunkered or on overwatch. Flank to avoid it.
  • Information: gunfire and explosions out of sight within 14 tiles of your team show as ripples, edge arrows and minimap pings, and enemies leave a “?” marker where you last saw, heard or sensed them, fading over 2 rounds. The Scout Drone reveals an area until the end of next round; the Motion Sensor pings enemies that move near it for 4 rounds.
  • Grenades and other throwables cost 1 AP. Shooting or throwing a grenade before you move leaves 1 AP for a move; attacking after a move, overwatch and hunker end your turn. The railgun and gauss rifle need both AP.
  • Every weapon has an optimal range where its aim is best.
  • Every operative carries a secondary. Knives, axes and blades strike an adjacent enemy (the machete and Monoblade dash a few tiles to reach one); throwing weapons can also be thrown a few tiles, a limited number of times per match; pistols have their own magazine and range. Secondary attacks follow the shooting rules: before moving they cost 1 AP and leave a move, after moving (or after a dash) they end your turn.
  • At levels 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15 your class unlocks a perk tier. Pick one of two class perks per tier in Loadout and change them between matches; some are new actions with cooldowns. The separate Perk slot works with any class.
  • Starter gear is ready at level 1; new weapons, armor, Mk upgrades and perks unlock as you level (see Loadout and Progress).
  • The storm shrinks every other round. Once the safe zone is small it stops shrinking and starts moving: the next zone is marked with a white ring (and on the minimap) two rounds ahead, then the storm slides there. Storm damage keeps rising, so keep moving with it. Supply drops are announced a round early; the pod holds the best loot in the match.
  • The strip under the top bar shows the turn order for this round, then the round break with any storm or supply event. You are highlighted, teammates are green, and enemies you cannot see show as “?”.
  • Duos: you drop with an AI partner. A downed operative bleeds out in 2 turns unless an adjacent teammate revives them.
  • AI opponents carry gear matched to your level and only know what they can see or hear. Pick Recruit, Veteran or Elite on the Play screen (the host picks it online): it changes their aim, gear, memory, hearing and how cleverly they flank. An AI partner always plays Veteran.
  • Six regions: Neo-Kowloon, Frostfall Station, Ember Wastes, Verdant Ruins, Ironhaven Docks and Blackwell Refinery. The map roulette after Deploy picks one you did not just play.
  • Play online: one player hosts a room and shares the 5-letter code or invite link. Friends join from Play online; AI fills empty slots. If someone drops, AI takes over their operative.
  • Matches pay XP and credits. Levels unlock gear upgrades; credits and shards buy cosmetics.
